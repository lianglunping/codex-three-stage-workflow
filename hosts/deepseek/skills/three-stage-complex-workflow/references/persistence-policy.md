# Decision-Rationale Persistence Policy

Read when a durable decision record materially helps continuation, provenance
or review. Length alone does not justify another document.

Reuse the actual project's plan, run ledger or decision log. Keep the objective,
relevant evidence, chosen approach, material assumptions, scope, acceptance and
unresolved decisions in one authoritative location. Link supporting artifacts
instead of duplicating them. Record only concise decisions and their evidence.
Never record hidden chain-of-thought.

The proposal alone does not authorize a write. Task-owned records already
included in the user's scope can be maintained without repeat approval. A new
destination outside that scope needs applicable authority. Do not write to
memory merely because a decision might be useful later; follow its separate
explicit authorization rule. Do not impose thesis folders, fixed character
thresholds or a prescribed announcement on unrelated tasks.
